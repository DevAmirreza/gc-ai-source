# gc-ai-source
## Government of Canada Open Data (CSV, 2018-2024): Grants, Contributions, and Contracts

This repository contains open data published by the Government of Canada in CSV format, covering the years 2018 to 2024. It includes comprehensive datasets for:
Grants and Contributions: Details on all grants and contributions awarded by the Government of Canada during this period.
Contracts: Information on all contracts issued by the Government of Canada between 2018 and 2024.
This repository aims to provide a centralized and easily accessible source for researchers, analysts, and the public to explore government spending and activities. All data is in CSV format for straightforward analysis and integration with various tools.
